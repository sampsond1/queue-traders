YOUR PARK-DAY STRATEGY

How to play

Watch the waits, make a few trades, and see how your park portfolio does. Your portfolio also lets you filter the graph to rides you own.

  1. 1
    Get your park pass

    Sign in or create an account. Start with 1,000 credits, then claim 100 daily credits the first time you visit each UTC day.

  2. 2
    Pick a ride

    Select a ride in the watchlist. Its latest operating wait sets the price in credits per share; zero-minute waits cost 1 credit.

  3. 3
    Buy low, sell high

    Choose a whole number of shares and buy or sell. Trades use the newest wait when submitted, and you can’t spend credits you don’t have or sell shares you don’t own.

Keep an eye on ride status: If a ride is not operating, trading pauses. A newly reported non-operating status wipes all shares in that ride. Your leaderboard score is your credits plus the current value of your open shares.

A LITTLE PIPELINE, DOING ITS THING

New observations arrive while you watch.

The chart reads public, read-only data from Supabase. A scheduled Edge Function collects ride waits about once a minute; the page checks for new rows every 60 seconds. Historical averages and reliability come from the saved Rope Drop News summaries.
